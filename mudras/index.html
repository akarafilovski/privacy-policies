Privacy Policy for Mudras

Effective October 11, 2026

Short version: Mudras keeps everything you do on your device and has no accounts. It shows ads from Google AdMob, which may use your advertising ID, and it downloads a small public list of our other apps. Everything below explains exactly why.

What the app stores

Mudras keeps a local, on-device database of which mudras you've marked as favorites. This data:

  • never leaves your device
  • is not linked to your identity, an account, or any other person's data
  • is deleted automatically if you uninstall the app

There is no account system and no sign-in.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts and no sign-in
  • No ads that interrupt a guide: only the ads described below
  • No location, contacts, camera, or microphone access
  • No tracking: the only network request is the public list of our other apps (see below)

Ads

Mudras shows ads from Google AdMob. To show ads, AdMob may use the advertising ID of your device and approximate location derived from your connection, and it measures how ads perform. Where required (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), the app first shows Google's consent form so you can choose. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Google processes this data under its own Privacy Policy and the AdMob data policy.

The list of our other apps

The About screen shows a list of our other apps and games. To keep that list up to date without an app update, Mudras downloads one small public text file from our website (axarapps.github.io, hosted by GitHub Pages) and keeps a copy on your device. The request carries no account, no identifier of yours and no data from the app. Like any web request, the host can see the IP address it comes from; we do not log or read it. Tapping a row opens that app's Google Play page.

Search and Share buttons

Two buttons in the app hand off to software you already have installed, rather than doing anything themselves:

  • Search image opens your device's web browser with a search query (the mudra's name). From that point you're on the open web, governed by your browser's and search engine's own privacy policy — Mudras sends nothing beyond the search text itself.
  • Share opens your device's normal share sheet so you can send a mudra's name and a benefit to an app of your choosing. Mudras does not see or record who or where you share it to.

Optional daily reminder

If you turn on the daily practice reminder, Mudras schedules a local notification directly on your device. No reminder data is sent anywhere, and nothing is scheduled unless you explicitly enable it in Settings.

Permissions

  • Internet: used by AdMob and the list of our other apps.
  • Advertising ID: used by AdMob, as described above.

Children's privacy

Mudras does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age, because it does not collect information from anyone. There is nothing to opt out of.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the update will be posted on this same page with a new effective date above.
