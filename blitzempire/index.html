Privacy Policy for Blitz Empire

Effective October 15, 2026

Short version: Blitz Empire has no accounts and no sign-up. Your settings and progress stay on your device. When you play with friends, a name you choose and a random code for your install are shared with the other players in your room, and online rooms pass through a relay server that only forwards game data and stores nothing. The app can show optional rewarded video ads (Google AdMob), can sign you in to Google Play Games, and uses Firebase for anonymous usage and crash reports.

What the app stores on your device

Blitz Empire saves your settings (sound, music, vibration, the look of the army, your chosen options for a match), your best score and the number of matches you played, the name you typed for rooms, and a random identifier for this installation (explained below). If you leave an online match by accident, the app also remembers the room code and your name for up to fifteen minutes, so that it can offer a way back into that match. All of this stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Playing with friends

The name you type. When you create or join a room you can enter a name of up to 12 characters. It is shown to the other players in that room. It does not need to be your real name, and we do not ask for any other personal information.

A random install code. The app creates a random code for your installation the first time it is needed. When you join a room, this code is sent to the host of the room so that, if your phone drops out of a running match, you can get your seat back instead of being replaced by a computer player. The code is random, is not derived from your device, your account or your advertising ID, and says nothing about who you are. It is not used for advertising or for tracking you across apps.

Online rooms. An online room works through a small relay server that we run. The player who creates the room runs the match on their own phone; the server only passes messages between the phones in a room and does not read, change or save them. The match itself (towns, moves, scores) is not stored on the server. Like any internet service, the server can technically see the IP address a connection comes from while it is connected, and the 4-letter room code of the room. We do not keep logs of players or of match content.

Rooms on the same Wi-Fi. The app can also play on a local network. In that case the phones talk directly to each other, and the app announces and searches for rooms on your network. No data goes to our server for this.

Invitations. The invite button opens Android's own share sheet with a short message and a link to a small web page that contains the 4-letter room code. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it yourself. The page is hosted on GitHub Pages and does not collect anything beyond what any web server sees in a normal request.

Rewarded video ads

Blitz Empire can show rewarded video ads from Google AdMob. A video is only shown when you choose to watch one. To show ads, AdMob may use the advertising ID of your device and approximate location derived from your connection, and measures how ads perform. Where required (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), the app first shows Google's consent form so you can choose. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Google processes this data under its own Privacy Policy and the AdMob data policy. When Kids mode is switched on in Settings, the app does not load ads.

Google Play Games leaderboard and achievements

Blitz Empire can use Google Play Games Services for a leaderboard and achievements. Signing in is optional. If you sign in, Google provides your player ID and display name, and your scores and achievements are sent to Google Play Games Services, where other players can see your score and display name on the leaderboard. Google processes this under its own Privacy Policy; you can manage or delete your Play Games profile in the Google Play Games app and in your Google account settings. The app does not send this data to any server of ours. Kids mode switches this off.

Analytics and crash reports

Blitz Empire uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. Analytics collects basic usage information (for example which screens are opened and how often the app is used) tied to an anonymous installation identifier that Firebase creates. Crashlytics sends a report when the app crashes, with the error and basic device information (model, Android version, app version). Google processes this under Firebase's privacy and security terms.

The list of our other apps

The About screen shows a list of our other apps and games. To keep that list up to date without an app update, the app downloads one small public text file from our website (axarapps.github.io, hosted by GitHub Pages) and keeps a copy on your device. The request carries no account and no data from the app. Like any web request, the host can see the IP address it comes from; we do not log or read it. Tapping a row opens that app's Google Play page.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: online rooms, ads, the list of our other apps, Google Play Games and Firebase.
  • Wi-Fi state and Wi-Fi multicast: finding and announcing rooms on the same Wi-Fi network. The app does not read the names or addresses of other devices beyond the game rooms it is looking for.
  • Advertising ID: used by AdMob for rewarded ads, as described above.
  • Vibration: optional haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, no sign-up, no email or phone number
  • No access to your location (beyond the approximate region AdMob derives from your connection), contacts, camera, photos or microphone
  • No selling of your data
  • No in-game chat
  • No ads that interrupt play: only videos you choose to watch

Sound

Blitz Empire creates its sounds and music on the device itself. It does not access your microphone and does not record anything.

Tip jar

The About screen can show static cryptocurrency addresses for an optional tip. They are fixed text bundled with the app: the app makes no network request for this and does not track tips.

Children's privacy

Blitz Empire is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents can switch on Kids mode in Settings, which turns off ads, Play Games and review requests. Players choose their own room name, so please remind children not to use their real name in a room.

Your choices

  • Delete everything the app stored by clearing its data or uninstalling it.
  • Reset your advertising ID in Android settings, or opt out of personalised ads there.
  • Do not sign in to Google Play Games if you do not want to appear on the leaderboard.

Changes to this policy
