Privacy Policy for Radio Greece

Effective October 2, 2026

Short version: Radio Greece has no accounts, no ads and no location access, and your favorites stay on your phone. It uses Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics for anonymous usage statistics and crash reports. Details below.

What the app stores on your device

Radio Greece saves your favorite stations, the stations you chose to hide, your recently played stations, and a downloaded copy of the Greek station list. This data:

  • stays on your device
  • is not sent to us or to anyone else
  • is deleted automatically if you uninstall the app

The station list

The app downloads its list of Greek radio stations from a file hosted on GitHub Pages (akarafilovski.github.io). Like any web request, this tells that server your IP address and the app version. We do not run a server and do not receive or store anything about you. The list is rebuilt every day from a public radio directory, and stations that stop working are removed.

Radio streams

When you play a station, the app connects directly to that station's own streaming server. Like any web request or media player, this shares your IP address and the app's name with that station's server. The stations are independent, and each is responsible for its own stream and its own privacy practices. Radio Greece does not control them and does not receive anything from these connections.

Analytics and crash reports

Radio Greece uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, both Google services. No sign-in is needed.

  • Firebase Analytics collects basic usage information, such as which screens are opened and how often the app is used, tied to an anonymous installation identifier rather than your name.
  • Firebase Crashlytics sends a report when the app crashes, so it can be fixed. A report includes the error itself and basic device information (device model, OS version, app version), not your listening history or personal files.
  • Google processes this data under its own Privacy Policy and Firebase's privacy and security terms.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to download the station list, play streams, and send Firebase analytics and crash reports.
  • Foreground service (media playback) and wake lock: so audio keeps playing with the screen off.
  • Notifications: to show the playback controls while a station plays.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No tracking across other apps or websites
  • No accounts, sign-in or purchases
  • No location, contacts, camera or microphone access
  • No selling of any data to third parties

Optional tip jar (crypto)

The About screen shows static Solana (SOL) and USD Coin (USDC) addresses if you would like to leave an optional tip. These addresses are fixed text bundled with the app. Radio Greece does not query any blockchain, make any network request for this, or track whether you have sent anything. Tapping the copy icon only copies the address to your clipboard.

Android Auto

If you use Android Auto, the car screen shows the same station list through Google's own media interface. Radio Greece does not receive information about your car or your trips.

Children's privacy

Radio Greece is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. The only data collected is the anonymous analytics and crash information described above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the update will be posted on this same page with a new effective date above.
