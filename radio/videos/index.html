Media playback foreground service

The radio apps (Radio Croatia, Slovenia, Bulgaria, Serbia, Romania, Macedonia, Netherlands, Greece, Montenegro, Bosnia, Turkey, Hungary) play live radio. The user starts a station, the app can go to the background or the screen can turn off, and audio keeps playing through a media-playback foreground service with a notification and play/pause controls. Playback stops when the user pauses it or the sleep timer ends.

TUNO: Radio, TV & News

TUNO plays live radio and TV streams chosen by the user. The user taps a station, the app can go to the background or the screen can turn off, and audio keeps playing through a media-playback foreground service with a notification and play/pause controls. The service stops when playback is paused or stopped.

Recorded on a real phone: playback continues in the background with the media notification and controls.
