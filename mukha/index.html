Privacy Policy for Mukha

Effective September 14, 2026

Short version: Mukha does not collect, store, or share any of your personal data, anywhere. Everything below explains exactly why.

What the app stores

Mukha keeps a local, on-device journal — your practice streak, stamps earned, favorited exercises, and weekly collection progress. This data:

  • never leaves your device
  • is not linked to your identity, an account, or any other person's data
  • is deleted automatically if you uninstall the app

There is no account system, no sign-in, and no server that the app talks to.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No advertising SDKs or ad identifiers
  • No location, contacts, camera, or microphone access
  • No network permission is even requested by the app itself

Daily reminder notifications

If you turn on the optional daily reminder, Mukha schedules a local notification on your device at the time you choose. This is handled entirely on-device by Android's own notification system — no reminder data is sent anywhere, and turning the reminder off removes it.

Timer sound

Mukha plays a short chime, bundled with the app itself, when a practice timer completes. This is local audio playback only — the app does not access your microphone and does not record anything.

Crypto tip jar

The About screen shows static Solana (SOL) and USD Coin (USDC) addresses, with a QR code, if you'd like to leave an optional tip. These addresses are fixed text bundled with the app — Mukha does not query any blockchain, make any network request, or track whether you've sent anything. Tapping an address only copies it to your clipboard.

Children's privacy

Mukha does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age, because it does not collect information from anyone. There is nothing to opt out of.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the update will be posted on this same page with a new effective date above.
