Privacy Policy for Tiny Toybox

Effective September 27, 2026

Short version: Tiny Toybox collects nothing. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no internet connection in the app. Details below.

What the app stores

Tiny Toybox saves only the parents' two switches, sounds on or off and spoken words on or off. This setting:

  • stays on the device
  • is not linked to a child, a person or an account
  • is deleted automatically if you uninstall the app

Nothing a child does in the app (what they tap, draw, build or hear) is saved or sent anywhere. Everything resets when the app is closed.

Permissions

Tiny Toybox asks for no permissions. It does not use the internet, the camera, the microphone, contacts or location.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No analytics or crash-reporting tools
  • No accounts, sign-in or social features
  • No in-app purchases
  • No links or buttons that lead a child out of the app: the Parent corner, which holds the switches and a link to this page, opens only after pressing and holding a padlock for three seconds

Sound and spoken words

The animal sounds are short recordings bundled inside the app. The little game sounds are created on the device. Spoken words, such as numbers, letters and colour names, are read aloud by the text-to-speech voice already installed on your phone. The app does not record anything and does not access the microphone.

Children's privacy

Tiny Toybox is made for young children. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, there is nothing to share, sell or delete.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the update will be posted on this same page with a new effective date above.
