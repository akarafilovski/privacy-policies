Privacy Policy for Koin

Effective November 9, 2026

Short version: Koin keeps everything you enter on your device. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no internet permission, so it cannot send your data anywhere. Everything below explains exactly why.

What the app stores

Koin keeps a local database on your device. It contains what you enter in the app:

  • transactions (income and expenses, with amount, date, category, subcategory and note)
  • categories and subcategories
  • funds, their balances and the transfers between them
  • monthly plans, budgets, yearly expenses and recurring items
  • settings, such as the currency label, theme, the start day of the financial month, the hide-amounts switch, the reminder switch and the PIN lock

This data never leaves your device through Koin. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts and no sign-in
  • No ads
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking
  • No internet access: the app does not request the internet permission
  • No connection to bank accounts, payment services, wallets or any network
  • No location, contacts, camera or microphone access

Backup files

Koin can save a backup of your data to a file, and restore from a file, only when you choose to do so. You pick the location with your phone's own file picker, for example Downloads or a cloud folder you already use. Koin does not upload the file anywhere itself. Anyone who has the backup file can read its contents, so keep it somewhere safe.

Separately, Android Auto Backup may copy Koin's data (your entries, funds, plans and settings) to your own Google account backup, if backup is turned on in your phone's settings. This is done by Android and Google under your account and your phone's settings; the developer of Koin does not receive this data and cannot see it. You can turn it off in your phone's backup settings. The PIN lock settings are never included in this backup. Restoring a Koin backup file does not change your PIN lock settings.

Optional cryptocurrency tip address

The Profile screen has an optional card that shows one fixed address for tips in SOL and USDC. The address is stored in the app as plain text. Koin does not connect to a wallet, a blockchain or any network, and it does not check or record any payment. If you want to send a tip, you copy the address and use your own wallet app. Tips are not required to use Koin.

Reminders

If you turn on the plan reminder, Koin schedules a local notification on your device. Nothing is scheduled unless you turn it on. No reminder data is sent anywhere.

Permissions

  • Notifications: for the optional plan reminder, requested only if you turn it on.
  • Fingerprint or face unlock: for the optional app lock, used only on your device.
  • Background work and starting after a restart: used to schedule the reminder and to add recurring items when they are due.

Koin does not request the internet permission.

Children's privacy

Koin is made for people aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including teenagers and children, because it does not send anything off your device.

Your choices

You can edit or delete any entry in the app, export a backup, or clear the app's data from your phone's app settings. Uninstalling Koin removes its data from the device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date above.
