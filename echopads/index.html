Privacy Policy for Echo Pads

Effective September 26, 2026

Short version: Echo Pads keeps your scores on your device and has no accounts of its own. If you choose to sign in to Google Play Games, Google handles your leaderboard data. Details below.

What the app stores

Echo Pads saves your personal best score, how many rounds you have played, and your sound and vibration settings. This data:

  • stays on your device
  • is not linked to your identity or to any account we run
  • is deleted automatically if you uninstall the app

Google Play Games leaderboard

Echo Pads uses Google Play Games Services for its leaderboard. Signing in is optional, and the game can be played fully without it. If you sign in:

  • Google provides the app with your Play Games player ID and display name so your score can appear on the leaderboard.
  • Your scores are sent to Google Play Games Services and shown to other players on the leaderboard, together with your Play Games display name.
  • Google processes this data under its own Privacy Policy. You can manage or delete your Play Games profile and data in the Google Play Games app and in your Google account settings.

Echo Pads does not send your scores, name or player ID to any server of ours. We do not run a server for this app.

Permissions

  • Internet: used only by Google Play Games Services and by Google Play's in-app review dialog.
  • Vibration: used for the optional haptic feedback in the game. You can turn it off in Settings.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No advertising SDKs or ad identifiers
  • No location, contacts, camera, or microphone access

If advertising is ever added in a future version, this page will be updated before that version is released.

Sound

Echo Pads creates its short game sounds on the device itself. It does not access your microphone and does not record anything.

Sharing your score

The Share button opens Android's own share sheet with a short message containing your score and a link to the app's Google Play page. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it yourself.

In-app review

After a few rounds Echo Pads may ask Google Play to show its standard review dialog. Reviews are handled entirely by Google Play.

Crypto tip jar

The About screen shows static Solana (SOL) and USD Coin (USDC) addresses if you would like to leave an optional tip. These addresses are fixed text bundled with the app. Echo Pads does not query any blockchain, make any network request for this, or track whether you have sent anything. Tapping the copy icon only copies the address to your clipboard.

Children's privacy

Echo Pads is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. The app itself collects no personal information. Google Play Games Services applies its own age requirements to signing in.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the update will be posted on this same page with a new effective date above.
