Privacy Policy for Radio Romania

Effective October 2, 2026

Short version: Radio Romania does not collect your personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no location access. Your favorites stay on your phone. Details below.

What the app stores on your device

Radio Romania saves your favorite stations, the stations you chose to hide, your recently played stations, and a downloaded copy of the Romanian station list. This data:

  • stays on your device
  • is not sent to us or to anyone else
  • is deleted automatically if you uninstall the app

The station list

The app downloads its list of Romanian radio stations from a file hosted on GitHub Pages (akarafilovski.github.io). Like any web request, this tells that server your IP address and the app version. We do not run a server and do not receive or store anything about you. The list is rebuilt every day from a public radio directory, and stations that stop working are removed.

Radio streams

When you play a station, the app connects directly to that station's own streaming server. Like any web request or media player, this shares your IP address and the app's name with that station's server. The stations are independent, and each is responsible for its own stream and its own privacy practices. Radio Romania does not control them and does not receive anything from these connections.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to download the station list and play streams.
  • Foreground service (media playback) and wake lock: so audio keeps playing with the screen off.
  • Notifications: to show the playback controls while a station plays.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services
  • No accounts, sign-in or purchases
  • No location, contacts, camera or microphone access
  • No selling or sharing of any data

Optional tip jar (crypto)

The About screen shows static Solana (SOL) and USD Coin (USDC) addresses if you would like to leave an optional tip. These addresses are fixed text bundled with the app. Radio Romania does not query any blockchain, make any network request for this, or track whether you have sent anything. Tapping the copy icon only copies the address to your clipboard.

Android Auto

If you use Android Auto, the car screen shows the same station list through Google's own media interface. Radio Romania does not receive information about your car or your trips.

Children's privacy

Radio Romania is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, because it collects none.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the update will be posted on this same page with a new effective date above.

Contact
